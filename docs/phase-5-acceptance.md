**Live notification gate passed:** On the updated HTTPS staging origin, a Chrome browser with a temporary persistent profile registered and confirmed a real Firebase device challenge. The synthetic staff-owned test device then received a real Firebase campaign message in the foreground; the browser showed its title and the test clicked **Open update** to navigate to the intended destination. The staging `campaign_test_requests` row recorded `provider_accepted` separately from browser receipt and open. The test-only open is intentionally excluded from customer campaign click metrics. The synthetic cafe was archived, test users were removed, and no active synthetic device remained. No customer audience was sent a message.

The hosted check also set browser notification permission to denied for a different signed-in synthetic member. The registration UI reported that notifications were off, and the member then opened the persisted card with its checkout-code control available.

Earlier attempts in private Playwright Chrome contexts, on both localhost and HTTPS, failed during `PushManager.subscribe()` with “Registration failed - permission denied” even though `Notification.permission` said `granted`. The persistent browser profile resolved that browser-test limitation; the service's acceptance evidence is the successful HTTPS run above.

Broader Android/iPhone installation, persistent worker hosting, production environment separation and launch load/security checks remain in Phases 8–9.
